How it works

First, a detector

A CenterNet-style network with 4.5 million weights looks at the photo at 416×416 and marks where items are. It does not care what they are.

Then, a classifier

Each box is cut out and shown to a small ResNet with 4.0 million weights, in four rotations. It answers: glass bottle, paper cup, plastic bottle or metal can.
